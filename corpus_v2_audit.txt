=== reproducibility note ===
v1 and v2 rollouts on the SAME init state are not identical. Checked directly:
v1 task04_ep00 and v2 task04_trial00 (both init state 0) emit bit-identical
executed actions for 66 policy steps, then diverge; v1 succeeded at step 249,
v2 never succeeds. Divergence coincides with an x-dimension top-2 logit gap of
1.0, inside the fp-nondeterminism band v1 itself measured between its own two
forward passes (mean 0.75, max 1.375). 66 identical steps rules out any
systematic wiring difference. Greedy decoding is deterministic given identical
logits, but the logits are not bitwise reproducible across execution contexts,
and one flipped argmax at a near-tie compounds through the physics.

Consequences:
  * "Re-running a consumed init-state index produces a byte-identical rollout"
    is false. It produces a shared-prefix rollout that then diverges. Init-state
    indices are still not independent samples, so the unconsumed-index rule for
    extension batches stands.
  * "The v2 corpus is a strict superset of v1" is false. Truncating v2 at
    t_success does not reproduce v1 trajectories. v2 supersedes v1; the two
    corpora must not be pooled or compared rollout-by-rollout.
  * Internal validity of v2 is unaffected: 300 distinct init states, all
    captured in one execution context.
